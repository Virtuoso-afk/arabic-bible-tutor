# Product vision

## Purpose

Build an Arabic-first learning platform that helps learners understand, practise, and reflect through structured courses and responsible AI assistance. The first vertical explores Bible-based Arabic learning, while the foundation remains useful for other subjects, resources, and teaching methods.

The provisional product name **مدار** is a working label. It should be validated with Arabic-speaking learners and teachers before brand investment.

## Product question to resolve

The first vertical currently has two plausible teaching models:

1. **Teach Arabic through Bible passages.** Learning outcomes focus on language skills; passages are licensed instructional resources.
2. **Teach the Bible in Arabic.** Learning outcomes focus on biblical knowledge; Arabic is the delivery language.

Mission 00A does not choose between them. Research must identify the primary learner, their goal, the teacher's role, the appropriate learning outcomes, and the content rights available. The domain model therefore separates a course's subject and learning outcomes from its language and resources.

## Principles

- **Arabic first:** RTL layout, readable Arabic typography, culturally aware language, and Arabic usability testing are defaults.
- **Pedagogy before automation:** AI may support an explicit learning objective; it does not define the curriculum.
- **Trust through clarity:** Label generated assistance, expose its limits, and let learners return to approved sources.
- **Licensed content only:** Use public-domain, licensed, or original resources with recorded provenance and terms.
- **Accessible by default:** Design for keyboard, screen readers, reduced motion, small screens, and variable connectivity.
- **Small and testable:** Prove learning value with a narrow pilot before adding classrooms, media, billing, or collaboration.

## Foundation concepts

- **Course:** an ordered learning experience with a subject, audience, language, and outcomes.
- **Lesson:** a unit within a course containing objectives and activities.
- **Resource:** a licensed or original item referenced by lessons, with provenance metadata.
- **Tutoring session:** a future guided interaction associated with a learner, course context, and safety policy.
- **Learning progress:** evidence that a learner attempted or completed an activity; it is not a claim of mastery by itself.

These concepts support language instruction, Bible education, and future subjects without encoding one interpretation in the platform core.

## Mission 00A outcome

The first interface communicates the intended tone and learning loop. It contains no copyrighted passages, real AI, accounts, persistence, or misleading controls. Its purpose is to make product and architecture decisions reviewable.
